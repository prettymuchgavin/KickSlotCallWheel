🌟 What is Gold Spin?

Gold Spin adds a special golden segment to your wheel.

When landed on, it filters out high-odds contestants (like Subs/VIPs with extra ticket multipliers) and triggers an underdog re-spin with gold animations & confetti so viewers with lower odds get a fun, fair chance to win!

🛡️ Provably Fair & Open Source

Transparency and integrity are fundamental to a fair giveaway system. Because this tool was created by an active member of the Kick community (GavinGambleGobble), complete open transparency is essential to earn your trust.

To guarantee 100% random selection and eliminate any possibility of manipulation or bias, the full source code for this application is completely open-source on GitHub. Anyone can audit the random selection algorithms, inspect entry handling, or fork and host their own independent version for total peace of mind.
